# JPCS-TSU Code Sprint: Design Direction

## Identity
Race-day broadcast energy, with school pride behind it. Everything reads like a live motorsport telecast: motion blur, light flares, checkered finish lines, "Trackside Report" segments. Underneath, it's still a student org event.

## Personality
A hyped pit-lane reporter. Confident, fast, and clipped, with short urgent lines. It's friendly and playful rather than corporate, and always pushing you forward.

## Palette
- **Core 1:** Racing Red `#D92B1F` (The red half of the logo ring and the "JPCS" letters).
- **Core 2:** Signal Gold `#F5B50A` (The gold half of the ring).
- **Bridge:** Hot Orange `#F27A1A` (Use when blending red and gold in gradients).
- **Background 1:** Overexposed Cream `#FFF7EA` (The blown-out white light that dominates the logo).
- **Background 2:** Carbon `#15110F` (A warm near-black for text and dark contrast sections).
- **Accent:** Broadcast Pink `#E6195B` (Use sparingly, for tags, labels, and one CTA at most).

## Typography
- **Wide Oblique Headings:** Syncopate or Michroma (Orbitron italic as fallback). Used for "TRACKSIDE" and section headers.
- **Condensed Impact Words:** Bebas Neue or Anton. Used for "PRIZES", "1st Place", etc.
- **Body:** Barlow or Inter. Clean and highly readable.

## Mood
Kinetic, bright, and a little retro. Motion blur, speed streaks, light leaks, film grain, checkerboard floors. High-energy with a nostalgic broadcast feel. The cream-white blown-out look is the signature, with dark Carbon variants for contrast sections.

## Dials (R-37 / Part 3)
- **ENERGY:** 3 (Bold). High-impact typography, high contrast, dramatic broadcast graphics.
- **RHYTHM:** 3 (Varied). Alternates between blown-out cream hero sections and dark Carbon broadcast segments.
- **MOTION:** 2 (Balanced). Motion blur effects, hover states, and a ticking countdown. Kinetic but not chaotic.

## Competition Compliance (JPCS Code Sprint Brief)
- **Theme:** F1 motorsport broadcast identity (checkered patterns, lap timelines, podium, live ticker).
- **JPCS Identity:** Integrated through the brand colors (Racing Red, Signal Gold, Overexposed Cream, Carbon) and the JPCS-TSU wordmark.
- **Content:** Professional, academic-focused, and appropriate for students, faculty, and the general public.
- **Originality:** 100% original code and layout. No templates or plagiarized assets.
- **Technical:** Vanilla HTML/CSS/JS. Responsive across breakpoints. Keyboard navigable. Deployed to [INSERT DEPLOYMENT URL HERE].

## Design Update: Brutalist Editorial
**Reason for Pivot:** After reviewing the JPCS originality rules and the desire to push the "extreme" design further, I pivoted from a "warm broadcast" to a "stark editorial" dark theme. This uses high-contrast black, white, and red to mimic the aggressive, technical feel of F1 engineering documents and telemetry screens.

- **Palette:** Carbon (Main Background), Racing Red (Accent/Lines), Cream (Text/Highlights).
- **Typography:** Extreme scale contrast. Massive, tightly tracked headlines paired with technical, monospaced body text.
- **Layout:** Asymmetrical grids, overlapping elements, stark borders.
